About Witspark

Witspark is a private, gamified companion for building self-control — not a public social network. Your journey is yours alone: no public feed, no public profile, your journal stays private.

Published by Luximo Labs by Lakshmeshwar Pandey, Witspark helps you stay consistent with a live streak timer, milestones & levels, and a powerful Urge Surfer — a 60 / 90 / 180s panic-button breathing tool for when cravings hit.

  • 🔥 Live streak timer with milestones and levels — watch your progress grow daily.
  • 🌊 Urge Surfer — 60s/90s/180s breathing when urges hit; calm the wave, keep the streak.
  • ⚡ XP, coins & reward shop — daily challenges, welcome bonus +25 XP +20 coins.
  • 😊 Mood check-ins + journal — XP once/day, reflect privately and track patterns.
  • 🛡️ Streak Shield & smart reminders — morning/evening/danger-hour nudges.
  • 📱 Android widget — home-screen streak at a glance.
  • 📤 Share Day-N card — celebrate milestones without a public profile.
  • ☁️ Optional cloud backup — start free as guest, sign up later.

100% private. Your data is yours — request deletion anytime, encrypted in transit. Self-help tool — not medical advice. If in crisis, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional.
