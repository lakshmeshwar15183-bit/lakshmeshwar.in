About RecallMe

RecallMe is a simple, private lend/borrow tracker that keeps every rupee, item, and promise in one place. Whether you lent ₹500 to a friend or borrowed a book from your neighbour, RecallMe remembers it for you — so you don't have to.

Built by Lakshmeshwar Pandey and published as Luximo Labs on Google Play, RecallMe is offline-first — your data lives on your device (Isar) and syncs automatically via Supabase when you're back online. No internet? No problem.

Why you'll love RecallMe:

  • 📝 Track anything — money (lent/borrowed), items, names, phone numbers, notes, amounts, currencies, due dates. Up to 3 compressed photos per record (private storage).
  • 🔔 Smart reminders — local notifications before due dates; one-tap WhatsApp/SMS reminder templates.
  • 📴 Fully offline-first — Isar on-device; SyncEngine pushes/pulls when online.
  • 🔒 Private & secure — encrypted in transit, no data shared with third parties, secure sign-in, delete anytime.
  • 🌍 Made for India, in 7 languages — English, हिन्दी, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, বাংলা, ગુજરાતી.
  • 📲 Share & save — export via WhatsApp/SMS/share sheet, save photos to gallery, dark mode.

Tech: Flutter + Riverpod + GoRouter, Supabase (Auth/Postgres/Storage), Isar Community, Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics/FCM — Material 3, offline-first sync, contact picker, image compression.

Who made RecallMe?

Is it offline? How does sync work?
Yes — offline-first. Every create/edit/delete writes to Isar first. SyncEngine pushes/pulls via Supabase when online. Auth via Supabase Email + Google (Web client ID) with app_links redirect com.recallme.recallme://auth-callback.
What languages and features?
7 languages (EN/HI/MR/TA/TE/BN/GU), currency choice, filters/search, contact picker, 3 photos, WhatsApp/SMS templates, local reminders, dark mode, FCM, gallery save.
Is RecallMe private?
Yes — Play Data Safety: No data shared with third parties, encrypted in transit, you can request deletion. Private photos bucket, account deletion available. Privacy policy: recallme-legal/privacy.html.
